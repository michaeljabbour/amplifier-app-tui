## Maintenance guard

`docs/audits/app-cli-baseline.json` records the reviewed donor commit and
`scripts/check_app_cli_parity.py` compares it with donor `main`. The weekly
`upstream-drift` workflow runs that read-only check and opens or refreshes the
existing drift issue when app-cli moves. A changed SHA is only an alarm: the
baseline cannot be advanced without another capability review and report.
